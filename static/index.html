THE WIZARD FACTORY

Every local coding-agent session (and every subagent it summons) appears here as a wizard. Agents running remotely on mage-tower appear as demons. Nothing registers itself — the server tails agent transcripts and infers what each is up to.

READING THE ROOM

  • Task desks — wizards sit here while working, thinking, or writing a reply. Each desk shows the session name from /rename; hover for the full label.
  • Desks appear when work starts and dissolve when the wizard leaves to wait or finishes.
  • Apprentice desks — smaller desks near the main wizard, when space permits.
  • Café games — waiting wizards play Magic: The Gathering, Go, badminton, or ping pong. Watch them chase the ball or birdie across the court.
  • The Magic table — duels run to the last life point: creatures fight on the mat, spells fly across the café, and Biggles judges disputed counterspells. Hover the table for the last card played.
  • Petition board — ! = needs your approval
  • Cafe — waiting on questions or your next prompt with a fresh order from Earl Grey
  • Hearth armchairs — idle 15min+, dozing off
  • Apprentices (smaller, beardless) are subagents; they vanish when their task ends.
  • Click a wizard for its journal: your prompts and its replies appear under counsel & missives.
  • Demons are agents running remotely on mage-tower; local agents are wizards.
  • Desk candles burn down as an agent's context window fills; compaction relights them. The bar under each roster portrait shows the same remaining context.
  • Sparks around a working wizard show reasoning effort: gold for max, violet for xhigh, blue for high.
  • Paper birds carry messages between agents, and from the tower when it continues a session.
  • Crossed wands link two agents that edited the same file in the last 30 minutes.
  • Ghosts by a CPU crystal are processes that outlived the agent that started them; hover the crystal for each machine's biggest draws.
  • Hover a usage bottle to see which agents drink from it.
  • The band under each roster row covers the last three hours: green busy, gold waiting for you, grey dozing.
  • Roster PR chips open Graphite and show CI: green passing, gold pending, red failing, violet merged.
  • Epithets are earned: rebasing, long builds, parallel apprentices, late nights, and so on.
  • Finished sessions become books on the laboratory shelves; click one to copy its resume command. ANNALS lists them with a daily chronicle of agent-hours, turns, and how long agents waited for you.
  • Hooded agents with a codex chip are OpenAI Codex CLI sessions (read from ~/.codex/sessions); one-shot codex exec runs finish and leave rather than waiting around.

SHARPER SIGNALS (OPTIONAL)

Polling sees everything within ~1s, but can't always tell a long-running spell from a permission prompt. For instant "needs your blessing" alerts:

python3 server.py --install-hooks  (and --uninstall-hooks to undo; a backup of settings.json is kept, applies to new sessions)
