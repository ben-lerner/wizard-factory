THE TOWER ROSTER

THE WIZARD FACTORY

Every local coding-agent session (and every subagent it summons) appears here as a wizard. Agents running remotely on mage-tower appear as demons. Nothing registers itself — the server tails agent transcripts and infers what each is up to.

READING THE ROOM

  • Task desks — wizards sit here while working, thinking, or writing a reply. Each desk shows the session name from /rename; hover for the full label.
  • Desks appear when work starts and dissolve when the wizard leaves to wait or finishes.
  • Apprentice desks — smaller desks near the main wizard, when space permits.
  • Table games — cafe wizards waiting for you
  • Petition board — ! = needs your approval
  • Cafe — waiting on questions or your next prompt with a fresh order from Earl Grey
  • Hearth armchairs — idle 15min+, dozing off
  • Apprentices (smaller, beardless) are subagents; they vanish when their task ends.
  • Click a wizard for its journal: your prompts and its replies appear under counsel & missives.
  • Demons are agents running remotely on mage-tower; local agents are wizards.
  • Hooded agents with a codex chip are OpenAI Codex CLI sessions (read from ~/.codex/sessions); one-shot codex exec runs finish and leave rather than waiting around.

SHARPER SIGNALS (OPTIONAL)

Polling sees everything within ~1s, but can't always tell a long-running spell from a permission prompt. For instant "needs your blessing" alerts:

python3 server.py --install-hooks  (and --uninstall-hooks to undo; a backup of settings.json is kept, applies to new sessions)

python3 server.py --demo --port 7778 fills the tower with fake wizards.
